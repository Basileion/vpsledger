Black Friday FAQ
When does the Devoster Black Friday sale run?
From November 20, 2026 00:00 UTC to December 2, 2026 23:59 UTC. Orders placed outside those dates are billed at the regular price.
Is the discount only on the first invoice?
Yes. The coupon takes a percentage off the first invoice of a new service: the first month on monthly billing, or the first full year on yearly billing. Every renewal after that is billed at the regular price.
Will my renewal price go up after Black Friday?
No. You renew at the regular price, which is exactly the price listed on our VPS and hosting pages today. We do not have a separate, higher renewal rate.
Which products does the coupon cover?
Web & WordPress Hosting: 25% off the first month, 50% off the first year. KVM VPS (USA): 15% off the first month, 30% off the first year. VPS plans ordered with a One-Click App or a game server get the VPS discount. Domains, reseller hosting and dedicated servers are not included.
Does the Black Friday code work on One-Click Apps?
Yes. A One-Click App is a VPS plan with the app installed for you, and the app itself is free, so the VPS discount (15% off the first month or 30% off the first year) comes off the whole order. Each app has a smallest plan that has enough memory for it; checkout only offers plans the app fits on.
Which game servers can I get on the Black Friday deal?
Minecraft: Java Edition, Minecraft: Bedrock Edition, Valheim, Terraria, Terraria with tModLoader, Project Zomboid, Factorio, Don't Starve Together. They run in the Pterodactyl panel on your own KVM VPS in Utah, USA, which suits players in the US and Canada. The panel is free; the VPS plan gets the Black Friday discount. One VPS can run more than one game if its RAM covers them.
Can I use the code more than once?
The coupon can be used once per customer account. It cannot be combined with other coupons.
Can existing customers use the Black Friday coupon?
Yes, on a new service. The coupon applies to new orders; it does not change the price of services you already have.
What if the VPS plan I want is sold out?
VPS stock follows the real free capacity on our servers, and that includes servers ordered with an app or a game. If a plan is full, checkout says so before an invoice is created, and nothing is charged. You can pick another plan or try again later during the sale.
How do I pay?
With PayPal or cryptocurrency at checkout, or from your account balance.
