Why We Put Every Black Friday Discount on Managed Cloud
Managed cloud is where ScalaHosting differs most from your typical host, so we put all our eggs in that basket.
Managed cloud hosting is a cloud server with dedicated resources where the provider runs the server for you. The CPU cores and RAM on your plan are reserved for your sites, and our team handles setup, monitoring, software updates, security, and troubleshooting.
Three things set our version apart:
Guaranteed resources. Your CPU and RAM aren’t pooled with other customers, so a busy neighbor can’t slow your sites down.
Control panel without license fees. SPanel , the control panel we build in-house, is included at no extra cost and doesn’t charge per hosted account, so adding a 20th site costs nothing more than adding the second.
Support that knows your server. In G2’s six-provider comparison, ScalaHosting had the highest displayed Quality of Support score: 99% from 433 responses , using reviews collected as of August 2, 2026.

Why Shared Hosting Is a Growing Security Risk
On shared hosting, many accounts share one server: the same operating system, the same web server, and often the same IP address. Each account is only as safe as the setup around it, and you have no say in how your neighbors maintain their sites.
That matters more every year.
Patchstack recorded 11,334 new vulnerabilities in the WordPress ecosystem in 2025 , a 42% increase over 2024, with 91% of them in plugins. The same report found that roughly half of high-impact vulnerabilities get exploited within 24 hours.
Here’s the thing.
On a shared server, a neighbor’s outdated plugin can get the shared IP blacklisted and drag down your email deliverability, even if your own site is fully patched. Entry Cloud gives you dedicated resources and your own IP, with blacklist monitoring included. On top of that, SShield , our real-time security monitoring system, watches traffic for attacks and blocks them before they reach your sites.
If you’d like the broader picture first, our guide to cloud hosting vs. shared hosting walks through the differences in performance and isolation.
When Shared Hosting Is Still Enough
Shared hosting remains a reasonable choice for a personal blog, a portfolio, or a hobby site that doesn’t collect payments or customer data. If your site makes money, stores customer details, or sends business email from your domain, Entry Cloud is the safer home at a Black Friday price close to shared hosting’s.

When the 1-Year Deal Makes More Sense
The 3-year deal isn’t automatically the right call. The 1-year deal is the better fit when:
You need a lower upfront payment. The 3-year term is billed up front. On Build #2, that’s $2,230.20 today versus $404.52 for one year.
