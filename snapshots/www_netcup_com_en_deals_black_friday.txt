netcup Deals - FAQ
What is Black Week?
Black Week is a special promotion week from November 24 to 28, during which we offer new deals and exclusive discounts on various products every day. Exciting offers from different categories await you every day.
When exactly are the deals activated each day?
The deals are activated daily at 8:00 a.m. CET and remain available until 8:00 a.m. the following day.
How long are the daily deals available?
The offers are only valid on the respective day between 8:00 am and 8:00 am CET on the following day. After that, they are no longer available unless they are offered again on Black Friday. On Black Friday, the deals are available until 8 pm CET.
Is there an overview of all planned offers for the week?
Yes, you can find an overview of all planned offers on our Black Week landing page. There you can see the product categories of the daily deals - but the exact products remain a surprise.
How do I recognize a flash deal and how long will it remain available?
Flash Deals are marked with a special flash-icon and are available for a very short time. Act fast, as these deals are limited!
Is there a way to be notified when new deals are online?
Yes, subscribe to our RSS feed to be automatically informed about new deals and flash deals.
How can I subscribe to the WhatsApp and Telegram channel for notifications?
Simply click on the link for WhatsApp or Telegram , follow the instructions displayed and join our channels. That way you'll always be up to date!
Is there an order limit per promotion?
In principle, there is no order limit per promotional product. However, a limit per customer may apply for some special offers. Please refer to the information on the respective product page.
Can the Black Week promotions be combined with vouchers?
Deals can be combined with vouchers under certain conditions. Exceptions may apply to individual products. If this is the case, we will indicate this on the product page. Please note any information on the product page.
Can I upgrade existing products/contracts to Black Friday promotions?
No, unfortunately this is not possible. It is not possible to upgrade existing products or contracts.
I missed the promotion. Is there a way to order the products afterwards?
No, unfortunately this is not possible. Unfortunately, the offers are limited in time. After the promotion expires, the products are no longer available at the promotional price.
Is the best price guarantee applicable to the promotions?
No, the best price guarantee does not apply to Black Week promotions.
Does the satisfaction guarantee apply to Black Friday promotions?
Yes, the satisfaction guarantee also applies to promotions for web hosting and root server products.
Does the price change after the first year or the first billing period?
No, the price of the promotion is permanently valid. There is an exception for necessary general price increases in accordance with our general terms and conditions.
