Who can use the Black Friday Sale offer?
During the 2026 campaign, new and existing customers can receive 50% off Shared Hosting or 30% off VPS for the first paid billing term of a new subscription ordered during the sale. Campaign dates: 13 October–30 November 2026. Existing subscriptions and their renewals are excluded.
What does the first paid term mean?
It is the billing period you pay for when ordering. A monthly purchase gets one discounted month. An annual purchase gets one discounted year, where annual billing is available. Later renewals are charged at the regular price for your selected plan and billing period.
Are domains or add-ons discounted?
No. The 50% Shared Hosting and 30% VPS discounts apply to hosting plan fees only. Domain registrations, transfers, renewals, separately priced add-ons, and setup fees are excluded. A free domain is included for the first year only with eligible Deluxe and Unlimited Shared Hosting plans. Starter does not include a free domain. Eligible extensions and billing periods are shown at checkout; domain renewals are charged separately.
What is the refund window for a sale plan?
Shared Hosting refund requests must be submitted within 7 days of the first purchase. Approval is not automatic; usage, exclusions, and the Refund Policy apply. VPS refunds are reviewed case by case and may be limited after provisioning. The sale does not extend refund eligibility.
How do I claim my discount?
During the sale, choose a plan from this page to open checkout with its code: BLACKFRIDAY50 for 50% off Shared Hosting, or BLACKFRIDAY30 for 30% off VPS. You can also enter the matching code manually. Only one code can apply per order; place separate Shared Hosting and VPS orders to claim both offers. Confirm the discount, selected billing period, and renewal price before paying.

The 2026 Black Friday Sale runs from 13 October through 30 November. Discount codes apply only to eligible new subscriptions ordered during those dates.
